# HRX-L3 — Statutory Leave Applicability Matrix

- Status: **OPEN — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** No jurisdiction is cleared.
- Gate: **HRX-L3** (ADR 0065 §19).
- Created: 2026-10-03, HRX.1 — Leave Foundation.
- Owner: the product owner, with qualified employment-law counsel for each
  jurisdiction.

## 1. Purpose

Lycenza's leave entitlements are **School-configured policy only**
(ADR 0065 §4.1, §22). The product makes **no statutory claim**: it
encodes no state or Union Territory leave law, and it does not decide
which law applies to a School or an employee.

This matrix records the questions that must be answered before any
automated statutory entitlement exists, jurisdiction by jurisdiction. It is
a tracking document, not legal advice and not a determination.

## 2. Rules for this document

- **Never mark a row `LEGALLY CLEARED`** without a recorded review by
  qualified counsel: name the reviewer's role, the date and the citation
  reviewed. This document has no such row today.
- The values below are **unverified placeholders** until a row's
  `Verification status` says otherwise. Absence of a known statute is not
  evidence that none applies.
- Applicability can differ within a single School: teaching staff,
  non-teaching staff, contract staff and staff of a government-aided
  institution can each fall under a different source of rules.
- A row moving to `VERIFIED` does not, by itself, authorize
  implementation. The `Implementation status` changes only through a
  reviewed checkpoint with its own ADR amendment.
- Product defaults (for example the April leave-year start, ADR 0065
  §22.1) are not statutory and appear in no row.

## 3. Status vocabulary

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Verification status | `NOT STARTED` · `RESEARCH DRAFT (unverified)` · `UNDER LEGAL REVIEW` · `VERIFIED (counsel, date)` |
| Implementation status | `NOT IMPLEMENTED` (configuration only) · `PLANNED (checkpoint)` · `IMPLEMENTED (checkpoint)` |

what counsel confirmed for a stated scope, never a blanket clearance.

## 4. Matrix

Columns:
- **State/UT**: the State or Union Territory.
- **Jurisdiction**: State or UT, plus any local overlay.
- **Board / recognition**: CBSE, CISCE, State board, IB/Cambridge;
  recognized, aided or unaided.
- **Establishment category**: for example a shop/commercial
  establishment, an educational institution excluded or included under a
  State Act, a government-aided school, or a minority institution.
- **Employee category**: teaching, non-teaching, contract, part-time or
  probationer.
- **Central law**: candidate central statutes to check; candidates only,
  not applicability.
- **State/UT law**: candidate State/UT statute or rules.
- **Service-rule source**: education code, grant-in-aid rules, board
  affiliation bye-laws, or the management's own service rules.
- **Effective date**: of the cited text.
- **Citation**: section/rule and the official source.

## 5. Cross-cutting questions for counsel

These are open questions, recorded without a presumed answer:

1. For each State/UT, does its shops-and-establishments (or equivalent)
   legislation cover a private school, and does that depend on recognition,
   aid status or ownership?
2. Do education codes, grant-in-aid rules or board affiliation conditions
   prescribe leave for teaching and non-teaching staff? If so, do they
   take precedence over general labour legislation for those staff?
3. Which central statutes (for example maternity benefit) apply to which
   establishment categories and headcounts? How must entitlements be
   computed, recorded and evidenced?
4. Are there minimum entitlements, accrual rules, carry-forward or
   encashment obligations that a School-configured policy must not go
   below? Does the product need to warn on a non-compliant configuration?
5. What leave records must an employer keep, for how long, and in which
   form? This interacts with E21-D9, the 8-year employment evidence.
6. Are there jurisdiction-specific holiday obligations (national,
   festival, local) that the staff working calendar must respect?

Health-related leave evidence is HRX-L1 and stays out of v1. The wage
effect of unpaid leave is HRX-L4 (HRX.5). Neither is answered here.

## 6. Current product behaviour (HRX.1)

- Leave types, policies, allocations and the working calendar are entirely
  School-configured. There is no statutory leave catalogue and no
  jurisdiction field.
- No entitlement is computed from law. No configuration is validated
  against law.
- Changing this needs a `VERIFIED` row (or rows), an ADR 0065 amendment
  and a dedicated checkpoint.
